# Contributing

The useful patch is one that makes `look.py --check` still pass, and that says which sitting CSV a home or a sign came from.

- Do not drop a trained policy in this repository.
- Do not relicense the Waveshare meshes. See NOTICE.
- Sign off commits with `Signed-off-by: Your Name <email>` (Developer Certificate of Origin).

Olivabot glue is Apache-2.0. Waveshare files stay MIT.
