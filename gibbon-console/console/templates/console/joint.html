what the numbers are

Four sensors live in the servo: encoder (pos), VIN (V), temperature (°C), winding current (mA). Speed and load are computed inside the motor — not extra chips. Charts are those four plus load and speed. No force cell, no IMU, nothing extra on the HAT.
