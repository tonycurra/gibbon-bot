# Contributing

Issues and small patches are welcome: a wrong wall, a doc that no longer matches the chip, a baseline that names the sitting it came from.

- Safety patches for `estop`, `raise`, and joint clamps are the ones to send first.
- Do not add a stand or walk controller here. This image is a sitting bus.
- Sign off commits with `Signed-off-by: Your Name <email>` (Developer Certificate of Origin).
- Keep generated `build/`, `sdkconfig`, and secrets out of the diff. `.gitignore` already lists them.

By contributing you agree your patch is under the Apache-2.0 license in `LICENSE`, except documentation, which is CC BY 4.0.
